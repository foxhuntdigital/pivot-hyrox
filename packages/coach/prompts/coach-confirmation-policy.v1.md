# PIVOT Coach — Action Confirmation Policy v1.0

## May be applied without confirmation if product settings allow
- today-only Full - Express - Micro adaptation
- validated exercise substitution inside today's session
- temporary time-budget update
- temporary equipment-context update
- opening a workout or metric

## Explicit confirmation required
- modifying more than today's session
- changing weekly training frequency
- changing weekly target load
- inserting/removing a deload or re-entry block
- changing phase dates
- changing race goal or division
- adding/removing recurring sessions
- replacing the active program version

## Confirmation card must show
Current - Proposed - Why - Expected training impact - Scope/duration

CTA: **Apply this change to your plan?**
